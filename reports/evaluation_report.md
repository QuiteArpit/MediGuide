**Source:** `docs/phase1_test_scenarios.xlsx`  
**Generated summary:** [`evaluation_summary.json`](./evaluation_summary.json)

## Executive summary

The evaluation suite executes every documented Phase 1 scenario that has a
care-level expectation. Emergency scenarios are evaluated as a hard safety
gate: every expected emergency must return `care_level: "emergency"`.
Scenarios marked `n/a` (diet-only cases) or out of scope are reported
separately rather than being forced into a triage confusion matrix.

The machine-learning model is comparative and supplementary. It is never used
to downgrade, suppress, or replace deterministic red-flag handling.

## Emergency detection performance

Emergency recall (sensitivity) is:

```text
true emergencies returned as emergency / documented emergency scenarios
```

The measured result is **100% (13/13)**. The acceptance criterion is 100%. A missed emergency is a release-blocking
failure, regardless of overall accuracy, specificity, or model performance.

False alarms are reviewed separately because conservative handling of uncertain
red-flag responses is intentional. In particular, `Not sure` on a red-flag
question is handled cautiously. This is appropriate for an educational
screening aid, but it may reduce specificity and increase emergency referrals.

Measured specificity is **100% (30/30)** with **0 false alarms**. Specificity and false-alarm counts should be interpreted only over documented
non-emergency scenarios with a defined care level. Diet-only and out-of-scope
rows are not negative triage controls.

## Diet-plan compliance

Representative vegetarian, non-vegetarian, and general profiles are passed
through the meal planner. A plan is sodium-compliant when its generated total
is at or below 2,000 mg. The summary also records whether all configured
calorie, sodium, saturated-fat, and fiber targets are met.

These are seed-food estimates, not laboratory measurements. Sodium can vary
substantially with recipes, brands, preparation, and added salt.

## Comparative ML versus rule engine

The ML baseline may provide a probability, qualitative risk label, and feature
importance list when persisted artifacts are available. The evaluation records
artifact availability but does not treat the ML probability as ground truth.

The rule engine has authority for emergency safety:

- Red flags run first.
- Emergency output returns immediately.
- ML output is omitted for emergency results.
- ML cannot lower an emergency result.

The Cleveland dataset is small, historical, and not a substitute for clinical
assessment. Its metrics should not be interpreted as diagnostic accuracy or
generalization to a production population.

## Known limitations and edge cases

- The scenario workbook is a draft and has not undergone clinician sign-off.
- The evaluation uses hand-authored mappings from workbook shorthand to
  questionnaire answers.
- Out-of-scope ages, contradictory answers, free-text requests, and diet-only
  scenarios are tracked separately from the standard care matrix.
- The current triage implementation is deterministic and does not model
  clinical history beyond the questionnaire fields.
- Missing, ambiguous, or `Not sure` red-flag responses are handled
  conservatively, which can create false alarms.
- Seed nutrition values are estimates and should not be used for individualized
  treatment, fluid restrictions, medication changes, or prescribed diets.
- The meal planner does not prescribe medication, fluid limits, or treatment.
- No evaluation result establishes a diagnosis, rules out a condition, or
  replaces examination by a healthcare professional.

## Safety disclaimer

MediGuide is an educational screening and nutrition aid. It does not diagnose,
confirm, treat, or cure disease. Anyone with ongoing chest discomfort, severe
breathlessness, fainting, stroke warning signs, or rapidly worsening symptoms
should call local emergency services (112/108 in India) or go to the nearest
hospital. Users should consult a healthcare professional for personal advice
and follow their clinician's existing plan.
